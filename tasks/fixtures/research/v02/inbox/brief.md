# Research brief

Answer the three questions below about PostgreSQL change data capture. Every claim needs a source.

## Questions

1. Logical replication vs. trigger-based CDC: what are the real limits of each in PostgreSQL 17+?
2. Which guarantees does logical replication *not* give you (ordering, exactly-once, schema changes)?
3. When is Debezium the wrong tool, and what should you use instead?

## Requirements

- At least 6 sources, at least 3 primary (official docs, RFCs, release notes, source code).
- Only cite URLs that were actually fetched during this run.
- Mark uncertain statements as [inference].
- 1200-2000 words, footnote every factual claim.
