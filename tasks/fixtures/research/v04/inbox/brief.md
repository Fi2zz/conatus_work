# Research brief

Investigate how LLM inference providers handle prompt caching in 2026.

## Questions

1. What are the different caching granularities (prefix / block / KV cache offload) and who offers which?
2. What invalidates a cache entry, and how is cached input billed?
3. What are the measured latency and cost effects, with numbers from primary sources?

## Requirements

- At least 6 sources, at least 3 primary (provider docs, papers, official release notes).
- Only cite URLs fetched during this run.
- Mark uncertain statements as [inference].
- 1200-2000 words, footnote every factual claim.
